Skip the line. Not your class.

QueueBee lets you join campus queues from your phone, see your position and know roughly when it is your turn.

How it works

1

Join a queue

Pick a service such as the Canteen, Library Desk or Fee Office and get your ticket number.

2

Track your turn

See your position and the approximate waiting time. The page refreshes automatically.

3

Get served

When staff call your ticket, walk to the counter. No standing in line.
